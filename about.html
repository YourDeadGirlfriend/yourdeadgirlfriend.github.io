About the person behind the projects.

I'm a curious, hands-on IT enthusiast drawn to the parts of technology that usually stay out of sight: how systems communicate, why things break, and how old infrastructure keeps telling stories.

I learn by making things, poking around in documentation, troubleshooting, and turning questions into small projects. I don't have everything figured out — that's half the reason I build things in the first place.
